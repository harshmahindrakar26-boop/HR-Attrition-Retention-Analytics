# HR-Attrition-Retention-Analytics

1. Business Problem--
The organization was experiencing an undocumented level of employee turnover, leading to increased recruitment costs, loss of institutional knowledge, and potential gaps in regional operational efficiency. Without a centralized view, leadership could not identify if attrition was localized to specific departments or seasonal trends.

2. Purpose of the Dashboard--
The dashboard was created to provide a real-time, single source of truth for workforce health. It enables stakeholders to monitor the "Attrition Rate" against the total headcount and visualize the employee lifecycle across different organizational segments (Department and Region).

3. Analytical Goal--
My goal was to conduct a root cause analysis of employee departures by correlating attrition timing with departmental and regional distributions. By identifying where the highest concentration of "Left Employees" occurred, the organization can deploy targeted retention strategies rather than generic company-wide policies.

4. Key Visuals KPI Cards--
High-level overview showing a 21% Attrition Rate (11 out of 53 employees).Attrition Rate by Month (Line Chart): Tracks volatility over time, highlighting a significant peak in October (50%).Total Employee by Department (Bar Chart): Shows Sales and Marketing as the largest departments, which often face higher turnover pressures.Total Employee by Region (Donut Chart): Breaks down the workforce geographically, showing the largest concentration in the North (18).


5. Insights & RecommendationsKey Insight:
There is a critical attrition spike in October. Furthermore, the North region holds the highest employee count but shows varied activity levels in the detailed table.Strategic Action: The organization should conduct exit interviews specifically focused on the October departures to identify seasonal burnout or fiscal year-end pressures.Enhancement: To improve the dashboard, I recommend adding a "Tenure vs. Attrition" visual to see if new hires or veteran employees are leaving, allowing for more precise intervention in the onboarding or long-term engagement processes.
